# Sales_Performance_Tracker_Dashboard

# Project Overview:
A dynamic Excel-based sales dashboard designed to monitor regional performance, product category trends, and individual seller productivity. It provides a high-level summary of financial health through key metrics like total sales and profit margins.

# Key Metrics / Insights:
Total Revenue & Profit: Achieved $208,771 in total sales with a total profit of $8,037.
Regional Dominance: South and East regions emerged as the highest contributors to total sales.
Category Performance: Sports and Home Goods are the leading categories, driving the majority of the revenue.
Sales Efficiency: Identified Eve and Alice as top-performing sales representatives based on total sales volume.
Order Fulfillment: Tracking order statuses across Completed, Pending, and Cancelled categories to ensure operational efficiency.

# Tools & Technologies:
Microsoft Excel: The core platform for data storage and dashboarding.
Pivot Tables: Used to aggregate and summarize large volumes of sales data dynamically.
Excel Formulas: Leveraged for calculating profits, margins, and regional summaries.
Data Visualization: Implementation of Bar charts, Donut charts, and Line graphs for trend analysis.
